## R scripts used for analysis and visualization of dissolved organic matter composition, dissolved organic carbon concentrations and greenhouse gas distributions in Malaysian peat-draining rivers.

## list of scripts:
- `Code_Tables_stats.R`: statistical analyses and summary tables (Tables 2, 3 and S1)
- `Fig_2_LO.R` — greenhouse gas and DOC spatial maps
- `Fig_3_LO.R` — DOM composition spatial maps
- `Fig_4_LO.R` — Van Krevelen visualizations and relative abundances of molecular formula classes
- `Fig_5_and_forest_cover_analyses.R` — Spearman correlations between remaining forest cover and DOM properties and greenhouse gas concentrations (Figure 5 and Table S2)

## required R packages are listed at the beginning of each script.

## related analyses
Figure 1 and Table 1 were produced using ArcGIS. ArcGIS was also used for the forest cover data referred to in the "Fig_5_and_forest_cover_analyses.R" script.
